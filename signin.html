Account Security

We take your security seriously. Here are some tips to keep your account safe:

  • Use a Strong Password: Choose a password with at least 8 characters, including letters, numbers, and symbols.
  • Keep it Private: Never share your password with anyone, even gym staff.
  • Update Regularly: Change your password every 3 months for added security.
  • Sign Out Properly: Always sign out when using public computers.
  • Report Suspicious Activity: Contact support immediately if you notice unusual account activity.

Need Help?

Common Issues

I forgot my username

Check the confirmation email we sent when you created your account. If you can't find it, contact our support team.

I forgot my password

Click the "Forgot your password?" link below the login form to reset it. We'll send you a reset link to your email address.

I'm getting an "Invalid credentials" error

Make sure you're entering the correct username and password. Passwords are case-sensitive. Try using Caps Lock to verify.

I still can't sign in

Contact our support team at support@gymadmin.com or call +1 (555) 987-6543. We're available during business hours to assist you.
