Frequently Asked Questions

Do you offer trial memberships?

Yes! We offer a complimentary 1-day trial pass. Visit our office during business hours with a valid ID to claim your trial pass.

Can I pause my membership?

Absolutely! You can pause your membership for up to 3 months per year. Contact our support team at support@gymadmin.com for more details.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express), debit cards, and bank transfers. Automatic billing is available for membership renewals.

Can I cancel my membership anytime?

Yes, you can cancel your membership with a 30-day notice. There are no hidden fees or long-term contracts required.

Do you offer guest privileges?

Guest privileges depend on your membership level. Standard, Premium, and Elite members can bring one guest per visit. Contact our staff for more information.

How do I book a personal training session?

Premium and Elite members can book personal training sessions through our online portal or by contacting our front desk staff.
