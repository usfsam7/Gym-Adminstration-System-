FAQ

Do you offer trial memberships?

Yes — a complimentary 1-day trial pass is available. Visit us during business hours with valid ID.

Can I pause my membership?

Absolutely. You can pause for up to 3 months per year. Contact support@gymadmin.com for details.

What payment methods do you accept?

All major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex), debit cards, and bank transfers. Auto-billing available.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Cancel with 30 days notice. No hidden fees, no long-term contracts.

Do you offer guest privileges?

Standard, Premium, and Elite members can bring one guest per visit. Contact staff for details.

How do I book a personal training session?

Premium and Elite members book through our online portal or at the front desk.
